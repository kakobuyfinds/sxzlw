How to use a HooBuy spreadsheet

  1. Identify the exact listing. Open the CAFANS item and confirm its original marketplace, item ID, color and size. Use the HooBuy option if it is available or try the original marketplace URL in HooBuy.
  2. Compare measurements. For shoes, record the stated insole length. For tops, check whether the seller reports chest width or full circumference and compare with a garment you own.
  3. Review warehouse QC when available. Compare photos of the ordered variant, size label and visible condition with the listing. Check the agent’s return or exchange deadline before approving shipment.
  4. Compare shipping quotes. Record the line, billable weight, price and what the quote includes. Use matching parcel dimensions and destination for each quote.

Frequently asked questions

What is a HooBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping index that organizes product finds and their source links so you can shortlist items before opening them with a shopping agent.

Do the listed prices include shipping?

The cards show reference item prices from CAFANS in USD. Check the current item price and add domestic delivery, international shipping, fees and applicable taxes separately.

Are the catalog images warehouse QC photos?

The cards show CAFANS listing photos. Check the warehouse QC photos for your actual item when your shopping agent makes them available.

What if HooBuy cannot open a listing?

Open the original marketplace URL and check that the listing is live and the variant is still offered. If it opens at the source, confirm that HooBuy supports the marketplace and the item before ordering.
